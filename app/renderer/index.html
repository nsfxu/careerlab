        Sync with efootballdb

        Real squad from efootballdb.com (same player IDs as FL26). Untick anything you want to leave as it is. Players FL26 doesn't have are created with their real ratings and a lookalike face.

        Sync a whole league

        Every club's real squad from efootballdb.com in one go. Players changing clubs inside the league are moved, never lost. Your own player is never touched. Untick a club to leave it as it is.

        Backups

        Every apply makes one. Restoring also backs up the current file first.
